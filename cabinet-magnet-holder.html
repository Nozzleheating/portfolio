Project 08

Cabinet Magnet Holder

Problem
The old magnet holder was not long enough to keep the cabinet closed.
Solution
CAD modeled a replacement holder with longer extensions so the magnets could reach and hold the cabinet shut.
Notes
Used Fusion 360 for this project.

Result

The CAD model shows the longer mounting extensions, and the installed photo shows the holder in place.
