Project 07

Bed Mount

Problem
Wanted to move Murphy beds to different locations on the wall.
Solution
Designed a clamping mounting device that fixes the beds to the wall and can be removed when needed.
Notes
Used Fusion 360 for CAD and FEA.

Design Iterations

Sketches, measurement checks, V1, and later V13 models show how the removable clamp evolved from concept to a working printed part.
