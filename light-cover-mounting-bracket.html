Light Cover Mounting Bracket

Problem
The light cover mounting brackets at our school were broken. There was no OEM part available at a reasonable cost.
Solution
Used a photo and measurements of an intact bracket to design a replacement in CAD.
Notes
Used Tinkercad and Onshape for this project.
